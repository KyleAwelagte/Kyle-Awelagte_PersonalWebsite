The person behind the art.

I'm Kyle Awelagte, a Zuni carver from Zuni Pueblo, New Mexico. Art, education, and running are important parts of my life, and each gives me a different way to learn and grow.

Where my art began

I began carving at age 11 with my father and brother. I mainly carve animal forms, paying attention to the shapes and details that make each piece different. Carving continues to be an important connection to my background and creativity.

College and running

I study Business Administration at Fort Lewis College, where I also compete in cross country and track and field. Balancing school, athletics, and art encourages me to stay organized and work toward long-term goals.

What matters to me

Respect, culture, and balance are values that matter to me. I want these values to show in how I approach my work, my responsibilities, and my future.

Looking ahead

My goal is to bring my interests in art and business together and help create opportunities in Zuni Pueblo. Building this website is one step toward learning how to present my work and communicate my ideas.
